Kaliningrad Region

Kaliningrad Region is Russia’s westernmost and most unusual region, surrounded by Europe and washed by the Baltic Sea. It is a land of amber, Gothic architecture, and sea winds. Here, the German heritage of Königsberg coexists with Prussian castles, knightly legends, and the modern resorts of Zelenogradsk and Svetlogorsk. The Curonian Spit is a unique natural phenomenon: A narrow strip of land between the sea and the lagoon, where dunes shift under the wind and trees hide the twisted shapes of the “dancing forest.” Kaliningrad Region is a place where you can watch the sunset over the Baltic, try amber honey, stroll along the cobbled streets of the old town, and feel how European architecture and the Russian soul create the special atmosphere of a borderland world.
