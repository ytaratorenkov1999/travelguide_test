Krasnodar Region

The Krasnodar Region is a true southern jewel of Russia, where the subtropical Black Sea coastline blends seamlessly with vast steppes, lush vineyards, and the dramatic peaks of the Caucasus Mountains. Here, it is possible to experience both the sea and snow-capped mountains in a single day – to swim in the warm Black Sea and wander through alpine meadows. Sochi stands as the flagship city of Russia's south – a vibrant destination of palm-lined streets, Olympic landmarks, and scenic mountain highways leading to Krasnaya Polyana. Yet the region offers far more than just resorts. It is a land shaped by Cossack traditions, rich winemaking heritage, fertile valleys, and ancient dolmens. Tea plantations thrive on its slopes, waterfalls cascade through the Guam Gorge, and on the Taman Peninsula, volcanic mud fields sit side by side with vineyards. The Krasnodar Region is where sun-drenched landscapes, sea breezes, and mountain air come together to create the feeling of an endless summer.
