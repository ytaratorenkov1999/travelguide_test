Leningrad Region

The Leningrad Region is a northern epic, where the Baltic Sea meets the granite cliffs of the Karelian Isthmus, and coniferous forests preserve the traces of ancient Finno-Ugric tribes. It is a true open-air museum: the fortresses of Vyborg and Shlisselburg, old lighthouses, Lutheran churches, the estates of Russian poets, and endless lakes, among which Ladoga stands as the largest freshwater lake in Europe. Here, history can be felt in the air – from medieval knights to the heroic defense of Leningrad. But the Leningrad Region is also a place of tranquility: fishing on quiet lakes, walking along pine forest trails, storms over the Gulf of Finland, and white nights that turn summer into an endless northern celebration.
