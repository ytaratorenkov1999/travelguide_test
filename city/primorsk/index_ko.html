Primorsky Region

The main phenomenon of Primorye lies in its name — in the word «morye» («sea») and everything connected with it: beaches, bays, cliffs, islands, the underwater world, and its inhabitants. The water element is contrasted by the Ussuri taiga with waves of green sopkas. The region retains the influence of Tungusic-Manchurian civilizations, and its capital bears both Russian and Chinese name: Vladivostok and Hǎishēnwǎi. Primorsky Region (or Krai) is a place where the waves of the Sea of Japan meet the cedars of the Sikhote-Alin, and endless forests hold the secrets of ancient tigers. This is a land of contrasts: Here you can find secluded sandy shores, bustling port cities, and untouched protected natural landscapes.
