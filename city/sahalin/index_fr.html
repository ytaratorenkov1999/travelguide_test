Sakhalin Region

Sakhalin Region is the only region in Russia entirely located on islands: Sakhalin and the Kuril archipelago. It boasts hundreds of kilometers of Pacific Ocean shores and volcanoes rising directly from the water. Sakhalin, Russia’s largest island (almost 1,000 km long), is separated from mainland Asia by the Tatar Strait and is washed by two seas: the Sea of Okhotsk and the Sea of Japan. It is an ideal place for active recreation: You can go skiing, diving, and even rock climbing here. Eco-tourism enthusiasts will find the island a true paradise. People come here to watch dolphins, whales, and Steller sea lions, explore the ocean coast, and enjoy the local cuisine.
