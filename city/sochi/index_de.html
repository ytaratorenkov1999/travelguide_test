Sochi

From palm trees and magnolias to eternal snows, from the sea breeze to mountain peaks. Sochi is a unique world where subtropics meet the Caucasus Mountains, creating a natural phenomenon of Russia. In 1838, the Alexandria fortress was founded here, giving rise to the future pearl of the Black Sea coast. Today, almost two centuries later, Sochi is the country’s main resort, the venue for the 2014 Olympics and world-class festivals, and its length makes it the second-longest city in the world. Here, sea routes and key highways leading to Abkhazia and the heart of the Caucasus intersect.
