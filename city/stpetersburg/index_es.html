Saint Petersburg

From the iconic drawbridges spanning the Neva River to the stately symmetry of grand avenues, from glittering gilded spires to the moody granite embankments. Saint Petersburg is a daring dream cast in stone — a place where human ambition defied the forces of nature. In 1703, Peter the Great laid the foundation of the Sankt-Piter-Burkh fortress on marshy islands, determined to «stand firm by the sea.» And so, the new capital of the Russian Empire was born. Today, more than 300 years later, Saint Petersburg remains the country’s cultural and intellectual treasury, and its historic center is one of the world’s greatest architectural ensembles. Here, the sea routes to Europe start, and the navigable stretch of the legendary Volga-Balt Waterway comes to an end.
